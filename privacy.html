HanaPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026 · Applies to the HanaPath website/PWA at cameronnel.github.io/hanapath and the HanaPath Android app (io.github.cameronnel.hanapath).

The short version: Your lesson progress, answers, and handwriting stay on your device. The website/PWA has no advertising or developer analytics. The Android app may show Google AdMob interstitial ads only after a newly completed lesson and never more often than once every five minutes. HanaPath does not send your lesson progress or handwriting to the advertising service. The current release does not enable HanaPath accounts or cloud sync.

What data HanaPath collects

The current release has no enabled sign-up, login, or account server. A disabled Google sign-in option may be visible while the owner completes secure server and Play configuration; it sends nothing. HanaPath does not receive your lesson progress, typed answers, or handwriting.

On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information needed to request, deliver, measure, and protect advertising. Google's current SDK disclosure includes IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app/user interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. HanaPath does not add lesson identifiers, answers, scores, handwriting, or local progress to its ad requests.

Google ML Kit may collect limited device/app information, per-installation identifiers, performance and feature-event diagnostics, and the configured recognition language. Google states that this information is encrypted in transit and does not include your handwriting strokes or recognition results. On the website/PWA, optional Transcript practice uses your browser or device speech-recognition service; that provider may process microphone audio under its own terms and returns a transcript to the page. HanaPath does not upload or store that audio or transcript. The Android package declares no microphone permission.

What stays on your device

  • Learning progress (completed lessons, review schedule, streaks, settings) is stored locally in your browser's or the app's private storage. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's browsing data, permanently deletes it.
  • Handwriting practice is recognized on your device. Your strokes are processed locally and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Progress backups (Settings → Progress backup) are plain files created only when you ask for them, saved wherever you choose. They never leave your device unless you move them yourself.
  • Ad timing stores only the local timestamp of the most recently displayed interstitial so the five-minute cooldown survives normal app use. That timestamp is not learner progress and is not sent to HanaPath.

Network use

  • Website/PWA: loading the site fetches the app and its lesson audio from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) to operate the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. HanaPath adds no advertising, analytics, or tracking to the website/PWA, and once installed as a PWA it works offline. If you choose Transcript practice, the browser may use its speech-recognition provider as disclosed immediately before the action.
  • Android app: lessons and audio are bundled and the learning paths work offline. When online, the app may contact Google to request AdMob ads and to manage any privacy/consent message required for advertising. If you use native handwriting, ML Kit may also download a Korean recognition model (roughly 20 MB). Recognition remains on-device. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure, ML Kit data disclosure, and privacy policy.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob interstitial advertising. An interstitial is eligible only at a natural lesson-completion boundary and only when at least five minutes have elapsed since the app session began or since the last interstitial actually appeared, whichever is later. Finishing another lesson during the cooldown does not queue an ad for later; the next eligible newly completed lesson may show one. Failed lessons, lesson replays that do not create new completion progress, and background/startup state restoration do not trigger ads.

The website/PWA does not load the native AdMob integration and remains ad-free. HanaPath does not sell learner progress or use lesson content as ad targeting data.

Consent and privacy choices

The Android app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request an up-to-date consent status at app launch where required. If Google indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, HanaPath shows a Privacy choices control in Settings so you can reopen the relevant Google privacy form. Ads are not requested in a production build until the SDK reports that advertising may be requested.

Children

HanaPath is intended as a general-audience language-learning app and is not directed at children. The final Google Play target-audience declaration must match the release configuration before public distribution.

Your choices

  • Delete all local learning data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's browsing data.
  • Use the bundled learning material offline; advertising cannot be fetched while the Android device is offline.
  • Use the Android Privacy choices setting whenever the Google consent SDK requires that option to be available.
  • Skip the handwriting-model download; the learning paths remain available.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published at this address with an updated date. The policy's history is publicly auditable in the HanaPath source repository, where the entire app can also be inspected.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised publicly via GitHub issues or sent to the support contact shown on HanaPath's Google Play listing.
