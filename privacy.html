HanaPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026 · Applies to the HanaPath website/PWA at cameronnel.github.io/hanapath and the HanaPath Android app (io.github.cameronnel.hanapath).

The short version: HanaPath does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you do in the app — your lesson progress, review schedule, and handwriting — stays on your device.

What data HanaPath collects

None. HanaPath has no sign-up, no login, and no server of its own. We (the developer) never receive, see, or store anything about you or your learning activity.

What stays on your device

  • Learning progress (completed lessons, review schedule, streaks, settings) is stored locally in your browser's or the app's private storage. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's browsing data, permanently deletes it.
  • Handwriting practice is recognized on your device. Your strokes are processed locally and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Progress backups (Settings → Progress backup) are plain files created only when you ask for them, saved wherever you choose. They never leave your device unless you move them yourself.

Network use

  • Website/PWA: loading the site fetches the app and its lesson audio from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) to operate the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. HanaPath adds no analytics or tracking on top, and once installed as a PWA it works offline.
  • Android app: all lessons and audio are bundled; the core app works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own. One optional exception: if you explicitly enable the experimental native handwriting-diagnostics feature, the device downloads a Korean handwriting-recognition model (roughly 20 MB) once, on-device, via Google Play services. Recognition itself still happens entirely on your device — your handwriting is never sent anywhere. Google's handling of Play services is described in Google's privacy policy.

Ads, analytics, and third-party SDKs

HanaPath contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no social or tracking integrations. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and the change will be reflected in the app's Google Play Data Safety declaration.

Children

HanaPath is a general-audience language-learning app and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it also collects no data from children.

Your choices

  • Delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's browsing data.
  • Use the app fully offline (native app, or the PWA after first load).
  • Ignore the optional handwriting-model download — the app never requires it.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published at this address with an updated date. The policy's history is publicly auditable in the HanaPath source repository, where the entire app can also be inspected.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised publicly via GitHub issues or sent to the support contact shown on HanaPath's Google Play listing.
