HanaPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026 · Applies to the HanaPath website/PWA at cameronnel.github.io/hanapath and the HanaPath Android app (io.github.cameronnel.hanapath).

The short version: HanaPath has no accounts, ads, tracking, or developer-operated server. Your lesson progress and actual handwriting stay on your device. The Android app uses Google ML Kit for on-device handwriting recognition and Google Play for an optional purchase; those Google services process the limited technical and store data described below.

What data HanaPath collects

HanaPath has no sign-up, no login, and no server of its own. We do not receive your lesson progress, typed answers, or handwriting. Google ML Kit may collect limited device/app information, per-installation identifiers, performance and feature-event diagnostics, and the configured recognition language. Google states that this information is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. It does not include your handwriting strokes or recognition results.

What stays on your device

  • Learning progress (completed lessons, review schedule, streaks, settings) is stored locally in your browser's or the app's private storage. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's browsing data, permanently deletes it.
  • Handwriting practice is recognized on your device. Your strokes are processed locally and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Progress backups (Settings → Progress backup) are plain files created only when you ask for them, saved wherever you choose. They never leave your device unless you move them yourself.

Network use

  • Website/PWA: loading the site fetches the app and its lesson audio from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) to operate the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. HanaPath adds no analytics or tracking on top, and once installed as a PWA it works offline.
  • Android app: all lessons and audio are bundled and the free learning paths work offline. If you use native handwriting, ML Kit downloads a Korean recognition model (roughly 20 MB). Recognition remains on-device, while the SDK may send the limited diagnostics listed above. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and privacy policy.
  • Optional purchase: the Android app can offer a one-time, restorable Handwriting Coach unlock. Google Play processes the purchase, account, and payment information under Google's policy. HanaPath receives only the product and purchase state needed to unlock or restore access; we do not receive payment-card details. Normal order records may be visible to the developer through Play Console.

Ads, analytics, and third-party services

HanaPath contains no advertising, social, cross-app tracking, or developer analytics service. The Android package includes Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition and Google Play Billing for the purposes described above. If that changes, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated.

Children

HanaPath is a general-audience language-learning app and is not directed at children. The same limited Google-service processing described above applies regardless of age.

Your choices

  • Delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's browsing data.
  • Use the bundled free lessons offline (native app, or the PWA after first load).
  • Skip the handwriting-model download and optional Handwriting Coach; free Alphabet block practice remains available.
  • Manage Google Play purchases and payment data through your Google Play account.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published at this address with an updated date. The policy's history is publicly auditable in the HanaPath source repository, where the entire app can also be inspected.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised publicly via GitHub issues or sent to the support contact shown on HanaPath's Google Play listing.
